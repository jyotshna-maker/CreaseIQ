# Plan review (Phase 0 exit)

This review challenges `PLAN.md` against the Phase 0 research (`docs/research_notes.md`) and a hands-on profile of the raw CSV. Each finding lists the problem, the evidence, and the change made. Changes that override the plan are also recorded as ADRs, and `PLAN.md` is patched where it was factually wrong.

| # | Finding | Evidence | Change made |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Python 3.11 floor can no longer be installed** with current libraries. | numpy 2.5.3 and scipy 1.18.1 declare `requires_python >= 3.12` (R6). | `requires-python >= 3.12`. CI runs 3.12 and 3.13. Actions are bumped to `checkout@v7` and `setup-python@v7`. See ADR-002. |
| 2 | **Tie rows include super-over runs and wickets.** The plan treats `team2_wickets = 12` as an anomaly to quarantine, but it is systematic contamination across all 16 ties: every tied innings total includes super-over runs, and one includes two super overs. | R8: e.g. MI 164/12 = 153 all out + 11/2. Rows 2015-04-21, 2026-04-26 and 2020-10-18 show the same pattern. | We do **not** quarantine these rows. We correct them: regulation scores come from `data/external/super_over_winners.csv`, and `first_innings_runs` and `second_innings_runs` use the regulation score for ties. A new column `super_over_winner` is added for analytics only; the classification target still excludes ties. See ADR-004. |
| 3 | **A voided and replayed match is mislabelled as "no result".** | 08-05-2025 PBKS v DC at Dharamsala was stopped by a security blackout and replayed in Jaipur on 24-05-2025. The replay is not in the CSV (R8, R10). | A `voided` flag is derived from a config list. Voided rows are excluded from scoring stats and from "matches played" counts, but kept in the raw table and the database. |
| 4 | **The `dls_flag` heuristic recovers only 6 of 16 D/L matches.** | The heuristic ("winner inconsistent with batting order") flags exactly 6 rows, all real D/L results. Ten D/L chases that were "won by N wickets" look normal (R8). | `dls_flag` = heuristic OR membership of `data/external/dls_matches.csv`. The quality report shows both counts. The heuristic is kept as a consistency check: every row it flags must be in the external list, and a test asserts this. |
| 5 | **Toss test design:** the plan's "chi-square of decision × outcome" conditions on a post-treatment choice. | Sood & Willis (2016) show the bat/field decision depends on conditions and team strength (R4). | The **primary** toss test is toss winner vs 50% (exact binomial with a Wilson CI). It is valid because the toss is randomised. We report the minimum detectable effect so a null result is read as "no effect larger than about 3 pp", not "no effect". The decision × outcome test is labelled *associational*. Venue-level tests use Holm correction. |
| 6 | **Baseline B1 leaks the holdout base rate.** The plan fixes B1 at "bat-second wins 54.7%", which is computed over all seasons including the 2025–26 holdout. | Section 2, finding 10 of the plan. | Every baseline rate (B1, B4) is estimated **only on the training fold** that precedes each evaluation season. The 54.7% figure appears only in EDA. |
| 7 | **Venue canonicalisation has non-obvious traps.** Beyond the aliases the plan lists: old Motera ≠ Narendra Modi Stadium (demolished and rebuilt); Mullanpur ≠ PCA Mohali (a new ground); Subrata Roy Sahara = MCA Pune; DY Patil is in Navi Mumbai; OUTsurance Oval = Mangaung Oval. | R2. | `configs/venue_canonical.yaml` maps 60 strings to 37 venue IDs, with coverage asserted by a test. Venue shrinkage keeps these grounds separate. |
| 8 | **Course fit risk.** The confirmed course is Machine Learning, but the likely VITyarthi course (CSA2001 *Fundamentals of AI & ML*) also covers agents, search, knowledge representation and unsupervised learning. | R1. | ADR-001 adds a small **unsupervised** component: k-means venue profiling with silhouette-based k. It frames Elo and hyperparameter tuning as a **heuristic search / local optimisation** problem, and the canonical YAML maps plus validation rules as a **knowledge base**. `docs/course_mapping.md` maps each unit to code. |
| 9 | **Stage labels differ by era.** The plan's labels (qualifier / eliminator / final) do not fit 2008–2010. | R10: 2008–09 had semi-finals and a final; 2010 had semi-finals, a third-place play-off and a final; 2011 onward uses Q1, Eliminator, Q2 and the final. | `stage` is derived by era: `semi_final`, `third_place`, `qualifier_1`, `eliminator`, `qualifier_2`, `final`, `league`. A test checks the derived champion against the R10 table for all 19 seasons. |
| 10 | **Home advantage in neutral seasons.** A constant Elo home bonus would wrongly credit "home" teams in 2009, 2020, 2021 and the 2022 hub. | R2. | `is_home` is derived from `configs/home_grounds.yaml`, which lists neutral seasons. The Elo home bonus applies only when exactly one team is at home. |
| 11 | **NFR-01 names an Apple M2**, but development and verification happen on a Windows 11 laptop. | Environment. | The benchmark records CPU and OS in `reports/perf.json`. Thresholds apply to the machine the benchmark actually runs on, and macOS is covered by the CI matrix. |
| 12 | **Player-name identity.** A few exact strings may refer to two people ("Harmeet Singh"). Many players share an initial and surname. | R2. | Players are keyed on the exact Cricsheet string and never collapsed. The ambiguity is documented as a known limitation affecting the experience features only. |
| 13 | **Secret hygiene.** A GitHub token appeared in plain text in the plan. | Launch conversation. | It was stripped before `PLAN.md` was committed. Authentication goes through `gh auth login`. `.gitignore` covers `.env`, and a pre-commit `detect-private-key` hook is added. |

## Scope confirmation

- Every P0 requirement is kept.
- P1 items are kept, subject to the section 14 cut line.
- The P2 Monte Carlo is retained as a stretch goal.
- The Streamlit `pages/` layout is kept, because R6 confirms it is still supported.
- Model families: logistic regression, random forest, HistGradientBoosting, a stacked blend and an Elo-only logistic model. XGBoost and LightGBM are excluded (ADR-002).
