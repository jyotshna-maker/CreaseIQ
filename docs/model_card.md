# Model card: CreaseIQ win-probability models

_Generated by `creaseiq train` from `reports/metrics.json`._

## Intended use
- Educational, pre-match estimates of the probability that one IPL franchise beats another, with explanations.
- **Not for betting or any financial decision.** The estimates are close to a coin flip by design of the sport, and the model card below says so plainly.

## Data
- Cricsheet-derived IPL match file (SHA-256 `db81a92c740ef6d9…`), decided matches only (ties and no-results excluded).
- Development seasons ≤ 2024 (walk-forward from 2012); **holdout 2025, 2026**, evaluated once per frozen selection (see `reports/holdout_ledger.json`).

## Method
- Features are computed as of the day before each match (date batches). Orientation is a hash-seeded coin flip, and predictions are symmetrised. Elo tuned on development folds: `{'k': 5, 'home_bonus': 75, 'season_regression': 0.1, 'use_margin': True}`.
- Tiers: **pre-toss** (history only) and **post-toss** (adds toss, batting order and the named XI).
- Families compared: elo-logit, logistic regression (no intercept, antisymmetric features), random forest, histogram gradient boosting, and a blend. Selection uses the one-standard-error rule on walk-forward log-loss. Calibration (none, Platt or isotonic) is chosen in time order.

## Limitations
- Small n (about 1.2k decided matches; the holdout has about 140), so confidence intervals are wide. Differences of a few thousandths of log-loss are not meaningful.
- Structural breaks: the Impact Player rule (2023), mega-auctions, relocated and neutral seasons. Relationships learned before 2023 (home advantage, rating persistence) weakened or reversed in the holdout years.
- Unmodelled: player availability and injuries, pitch and dew, weather, individual form, ball-by-ball dynamics.
- Franchise continuity assumptions (ADR-003). Some D/L results in 2010, 2012 and 2013 may be unflagged. Player identity is exact-string only.

## Ethical considerations
- Outputs must not be used for betting (Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025) or to judge individuals.
- The model predicts team outcomes only. It uses no personal data beyond public match records.
